Bain & Company interview process: candidate prep guide

Bain interviews usually have two rounds. Each round mixes case interviews with experience (behavioral) questions, and each conversation lasts about 30 to 45 minutes.

Interviewers look for structured problem solving, comfort with numbers, clear communication and evidence of impact on a team.
